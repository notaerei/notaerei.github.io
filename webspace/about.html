about webSpace & FAQ

webSpace — a space with no ends.

what is webSpace?

webSpace is a collection of my notes on several figures of interest found across the web. in other words, webSpace is a database of all my characters from multiple different projects and universes created by me.

what are your rules for using your characters in art?

an overarching rule is that if you are posting art (illustration, literature, edits, collages, etc.) of any of my characters online, you MUST credit me for the design. this can be done by simply linking to my carrd, or any of my socials, somewhere within your post.

concepts that are allowed and welcomed:

  • using outfits that are different than what is provided in the original reference
  • portraying platonic interactions with any other characters
  • changing species

concepts that need my approval first:

you are free to contact me if you want to check if something is okay!

  • nsfw portrayal for characters older than 18
  • portraying intimate or romantic interactions between any of my characters that aren't already canonically paired together
  • genderbending for characters that aren't genderfluid
  • extreme or excessive gore

concepts that are absolutely not allowed:

  • nsfw portrayal for characters younger than 18
  • portraying intimate or romantic interactions between my characters that are within the same family
  • drastically changing skin colour or body type
  • drastically aging my characters up or down
  • associating my characters with hate speech or discriminatory imagery
