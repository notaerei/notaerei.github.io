   ❝ WHO IS MADI ? ❞



MADI is a neurodivergent music producer, artist, and DJ based in los angeles, california.   throughout her late adolescence, MADI was involved in the electronic music scene in los angeles, where she devoted her time to learning music production.   with so much experience in the industry starting at a young age, a large majority of MADI's tracks are written, produced, recorded, and engineered by her.

most of MADI's music delves into her personal experiences with chronic physical and mental illnesses, which is (mostly) why her art resonates so deeply with me.   her music also explores themes of femininity, (platonic and romantic) relationships, and childhood.   her artistic eye and sense of style reflected through her music and fashion are also major inspirations to me.   MADI's blend of modern hyperpop mixed with futuristic experimental influence all wrapped up in poetic and raw lyricism makes for a wonderful listening experience.

MADI is a beautiful example of someone who is not afraid to freely express herself, reclaim her past traumas, and rebirth into someone who leaves imprints on the lives of others through staying true to herself.   MADI is truly a visionary, and i often credit her for being a major positive impact on my life since i started listening to her music!

also, the food she cooks on her cooking page looks so good omfg. #lethercook

  live laugh love MADI

a retrospective by ari.

2016 was a pivotal point for me when it comes to exploring my music taste.   not only was it the year i started exploring music beyond what i usually listened to (middle school emo classics and mainstream pop), but it was specifically the point in my life where i started getting really into electronic music.   these were the days where the "[insert genre here] nation" youtube channels were getting really popular, and i actually have a playlist i made back then of songs i discovered through those channels and enjoyed.   little did i know that by listening to songs uploaded by these channels so frequently, i would hear a song featuring MADI for the first time. i'm not going to lie and claim that i started to listen to her music religiously since then — in truth, i didn't dig too deep further past listening to the song a few times.

years later, in 2020, i stumbled across a tweet on my timeline from someone i followed referencing her album "forsaken" which had just released at the time.   curiously, that same night, i decided to listen to the entire album front to back on spotify.   i instantly fell in love with the style of "forsaken", as her style reminded me a lot of electronic music on youtube and soundcloud from 2016 to 2018.   it also kinda reminded me of the music and general vibes from american horror story: coven, which is my favourite season of american horror story, but i digress.   i added a few of her songs to my personal playlist, followed her on twitter, and left it that.

the hold that MADI's music has on me didn't truly start until 2023, when she announced the release of her album "DYING ANGEL" on twitter, which coincidentally was releasing on the day before my birthday.   in my personal life, 2023 was quite a difficult year for me.   i was more than halfway through university at that point, was going through a difficult breakup in the midst of an existing mental health crisis, and overall felt alone, depressed, and empty.

i decided to listen to the album, remembering that i already liked "forsaken" from years ago, so i figured i would like this album as well.   i remember sitting on my mom's bed making charm bracelets with some random spare stuff from my crafts bin, intending on listening to the entire album front to back.   when the first few notes of "LEMON JUICE" began to play, i had to put the damn bracelets down.   with that bass kicking in during the intro, the hats echoing through my headphones, the rhythmic design of the melody in general...   i was starstruck by the first song of the album alone.   by the time i got to the end with "DYING ANGEL," i sat there with my unfinished bracelet in hand, completely shellshocked.   needless to say, the entire experience was surreal.   so of course, i had to listen to it again the next day, on my birthday.

and then again, on new year's eve.   and again, on new year's day of 2024.   and again, the next day.   and again, and again, and again.   until i had been listening to the album front to back everyday for at least a week.   then i realized if i liked this album so much, i may as well show further support for the artist, right?   i followed MADI on every platform i could find, started listening to the rest of her discography, went down rabbit holes of her soundcloud page and youtube channel.   i quickly adored her unique audio and visual style based off of listening to this album alone.   i was so obsessed with this album that i would wake up listening to it, fall asleep listening to it, work my 8 hour shifts listening to it, listen to it commuting to and from classes or errands.   it was literally on loop almost 24/7 for months.   it eventually became one of my top listened-to albums of 2024 — no surprises there.

what fueled my obsession for this album even more was that i discovered after the first few times of listening to its entirety that MADI wrote it with the intention of sharing her experiences specifically with borderline personality disorder (among other kinds of physical and mental illnesses, but bpd was the one that stood out the most to me, because i myself have been diagnosed with it for years as well.)   once i discovered that tidbit of information, it suddenly clicked why i resonated so strongly with the themes of this album. you have tracks like "CUFFED" or "HORROR NITE" which highlight how fear affects and drives relationships, "THIRDEYE" which is such an accurate audio representation of bpd splitting, and of course "DYING ANGEL" which expresses the desperation and psychological pain that comes with attempting to seek love and approval while battling mental illness.   i couldn't help but feel truly seen by this album.   seen by this woman who has clearly experienced such similar traumatic life events as me, and can turn that pain into such hauntingly raw and truly beautiful music.

as i began to get a better hold of my life around halfway through 2024, i held "DYING ANGEL" very close to my heart.   it helped me reflect on where i was in life, and where i wanted to be in the future.   i was inspired by this album to keep pushing forward in life, to attempt to chase my creative hobbies again after a long period of creative block, and to learn how to trust and love people again.   after a lot of deliberation, i began to work on my own personal music projects again for the first time in years.   this actually directly led to me producing and releasing "gates of heaven" in one night as an attempt to transform my emotional pain into art, as i had been recovering from hospitalization(s) mere weeks before release and believed i wouldn't be surviving past 2024.   MADI's music also directly inspired one of my other singles, "sick," which arguably reflects my music style better than any of my other music.   while making "sick," i was really inspired by the sense of impending doom in some of MADI's heavier tracks, as well as her use of vocal splicing and distorted screaming, and how she uses her voice itself as an instrument sometimes.   but i digress, this isn't self promo, my bad.   my point is that her musical style really helped coax out my willingness to experiment with my own, and to this day, i'm still satisfied with how "sick" turned out despite my lack of music production experience.

i did end up surviving past 2024, clearly.   as i was finishing up my degree, i remember spending nights waiting for the amtrak at anaheim's transit station listening to "DYING ANGEL" along with her second EP with GLOWSTONE, but most especially her song "NO.1 FAN (la love)" which was released as a single at the time.   similarly to "DYING ANGEL," i was flabbergasted at how strongly i resonated with "NO.1 FAN (la love)."   it literally felt like MADI was in my walls, writing music about such specific events in my life.   down to the lyric "hope you have your fun on wednesdays, playing games and talking shit 'bout me" like girl how the fuck did you know my opps were doing this to me on wednesdays specifically.   HOW DOES SHE KNOW?!?!

anyways, needless to say, i was equally obsessed with this song as much as i was with "DYING ANGEL."   i would spend commutes on the bus or train listening to her songs over and over again, transcribing the lyrics in my notes app to pass the time.   at some point, i even contributed to the genius page for "DYING ANGEL" (the single) and intended on transcribing the rest of the album as well, but that may be a later project.

the release of her albums in 2025 came at scarily perfect times as well.   "LIFE ON HARD MODE (Speed Run!)" was released almost directly after i was grieving yet another painful breakup.   i remember at the time, i was showing music from that album to my friends because the range of genres was a bit more accessible to people who don't usually listen to electronic music, and i simply wanted to bring in more support for MADI's music, y'know, get those streams up.   ironically, even though there's a song in that album about how MADI doesn't have her driver's license, i was also extremely terrified of driving around the time the album came out (albeit, i did already have my license at the time, though.)   "backyard ghosts" was released just as i was crawling out of recovering from health issues, as well as trying to ease into my new living situation that arguably changed my life for the better.   although my life was objectively improving during late 2025, i was still deeply unsatisfied and unhappy with the uncertainty of the trajectory of where my life was headed, and it once again felt like my only escape from the mundane cycles of my day to day life were listening to MADI's music.   i was listening to this album while spending hours commuting to and from work, dreaming of a day that i could once again pursue my creative hobbies again without feeling like i was missing out on supporting myself.

i'm aware this next part is going to sound a little silly.   but in a way, listening to MADI's music for years has felt like i was living vicariously through her.   through the ups and downs of my personal life, so many events, feelings, thoughts, fears, and vulnerabilities have been spoken through MADI's music directly into my own ears, while simultaneously being shared to the entire world as well.   in that way, i felt a little less alone when it seemed like i had no one in my corner.   i felt a little less scared, because i knew there was a woman out there who knew what i was going through, and was pushing to make it to "the other side."   i have never met this woman in my life.   but somehow it felt like she knew me, because she knew herself, and she knew how to express it through her art.   with many artists i have admired in my life, i find the relationship between creator and follower very beautiful.   objectively, there is none at all.   but spiritually, we as humans are all connected closer than we think.   there are so many people i will never meet, whose shoes i have walked in, and so many experiences i will never live, tied to the people closest to me.   that's a little terrifying to think about how many people out there are just like me, who aren't aware of it, and may never be.   but it's equally as gentle to think about how many people are out there that make themselves and their minds known, and allow people like me to find comfort in their expression of humanity.

as i've been following her throughout the years, it increasingly became a dream of mine to see her perform live someday, as the live performances she did while i was still in college weren't accessible for me at the time.   after she announced that she was taking a break from performing and making music in general to focus on her health and recovery from surgery, and as i was battling health issues myself, my chances of ever fulfilling that dream started to seem a little bleak.   that was until GLOWSTONE was announced as one of the performers for lost in dreams 2026 in los angeles.   i remember seeing this announcement on instagram and IMMEDIATELY setting an alarm to pre-order a ticket for myself.   mind you, i had no plans of where i was going to stay, how i was going to get there, who i would be going with (if anybody was willing to), or if i would even be available the day of the festival.   i did not care.   i had vowed to myself back in 2024 that i would see MADI perform someday, even just once.   because it almost felt like i had to fulfill a promise to the version of myself who was listening to her music simply to get through the day, keeping her discography around like a lifeline and feeling utterly hopeless.   although MADI's music has helped support me through the worst periods of my life, i wanted to create some positive connotation around her as an artist as well, in an environment that felt safe to my nervous system, all while celebrating my independence and recovery that i have worked very hard on over the years.

that brings us to now.   i started building this webshrine at around 1:00 PM on july 10 — it is now 11:23 PM, and i'm supposed to drive up to los angeles tomorrow morning before lost in dreams.   i am watching GLOWSTONE perform in less than 24 hours from right now.   i actually messaged MADI about me attending lost in dreams back in march, and to my BIGGEST surprise, she offered to meet me after their set which was a whole emotional whirlwind at the time i received that message, haha.   but now, i'm a little less nervous about meeting one of my favourite artists of all time, because as i'm writing this retrospective and reflecting on my experiences listening to MADI's discography over the years, i'm realizing that while of course her music has been a huge comfort to me, this entire time, the strength i needed to keep me pushing through was always inside of me.   i was always capable of braving through the scariest parts of life on my own.   for awhile, i used to make the mistake of crediting someone else for changing my life.   i'm now happy and proud to say that i was always there for myself, and it just so happens that MADI's music came along with me for the ride.   i'm forever grateful for that.

MADI, if you're reading this far, that means you've opened my letter, and you are officially in possession of one of the two bracelets i made on the night before my birthday in 2023, otherwise known as the night that i listened to "DYING ANGEL" for the first time.   i have the other bracelet.   at this point, i've already expressed how much i love your art from a "consumer" standpoint, and how you have inspired me to continue pursuing my own interests in music production, as well as continue facing the highs and lows of life itself as well.   however, i want to also express my greatest appreciation for your kindness throughout all our message interactions.   not only have you made me feel seen through your music, but you've also made me feel seen as a person through conversation during times where i felt i needed to hear someone outside of my circle reassure me that everything would turn out okay.

i remember during one exchange, you told me that your life is not the same as it was when you were producing "DYING ANGEL," and that it has improved in many ways since then.   it's funny looking back on this, because at the time, "DYING ANGEL" seemed like a scarily accurate reflection of my daily struggles.   i didn't genuinely believe it would "get better" like people say it does.   until it did.   and i finally got to experience what you were talking about.   funnily enough, now it feels like i'm living the secondary half of "LIFE ON HARD MODE (Speed Run!)," so is it just going to be a pattern that you're going to accurately predict events in my life through music releases or what...

jokes aside, i'm so happy, grateful, and blessed to be able to see you perform live, and i really hope you enjoy this little webshrine of mine!   since i was building this from scratch on a time crunch, i'm intending on sprucing it up a little more next week, so if you're still seeing this sentence, the site isn't fully done yet.   nonetheless, i am not exaggerating when i say that i think i will always be one of your biggest supporters, i swear, i never skip a single song that you make, they're ALL bangers and i (mostly) know the lyrics and melody to nearly every single one.   if i still used spotify, i would be able to prove just how many hours i have put into your discography, it's actually bordering insanity.

to everyone else who isn't MADI reading this, i highly encourage you to check out her music if you haven't already, especially if you already like electronic music and want something fresh to listen to!   MADI's discography covers such a wide spread of subgenres, but my favourite tracks from her are drum and bass heavy because i'm a self proclaimed bass slut, so if you enjoy that, you will definitely love her music just like i do.   if you're neurodivergent, or struggle with illness in any way, you will also love her music.   if you're navigating life, and want to feel like someone truly understands what you're going through, you will love her music.   if you're into fashion, you will love her fits.   if you're into cooking, you will literally smell her food pictures and videos through the screen, everything looks so damn good.   if you care about human rights, you will love her as a person.   every aspect of MADI's presence online is worth investing some time into exploring.   MADI is one of the most underrated artists of not only the electronic music scene, but of our generation as well.   i promise you, you won't regret supporting her.

live, laugh, love MADI !
